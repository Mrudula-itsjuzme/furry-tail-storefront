Description

The Refreshing Mist is a light coat spray for between-bath freshening. Not a dry shampoo — a true mist that settles into the coat and leaves a clean, lasting fragrance. Yuzu & White Musk is the lighter option in the Refresh family: cleaner, more citrus-forward, less enveloping than the Dry Foam. Use it alone as a quick between-bath refresh, or as a finish after the Dry Foam Shampoo. Three to five sprays over the back and flanks, allow to settle, done.

Key Ingredients
  • Citrus Junos Peel Oil (Yuzu) — fragrance lead. Cold-pressed Japanese citrus peel. Brighter than lemon, warmer than grapefruit, slightly tart.
  • White Musk Accord (synthetic, IFRA-compliant) — fragrance base. Warm, clean, airy. Lingers on the coat without overpowering.
  • Panthenol (Vitamin B5) — skin barrier. Light conditioning benefit on leave-in contact.
  • Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice — soothing. Light, non-greasy base.
  • Alcohol Denat. — carrier. Ensures fine mist dispense and rapid dry-down.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation.
How to Use

Hold 25cm from the coat and apply 3 to 5 sprays over the back and flanks. Allow to settle — do not rub in. The mist is designed to dry naturally. Use after the Dry Foam Shampoo as a finish, or alone as a quick between-bath refresh. Can be used daily.

Safe For

Formulated for dogs. Not suitable for cats. See our cat safety note for full detail on species safety.
