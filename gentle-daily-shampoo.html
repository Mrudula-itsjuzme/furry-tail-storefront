The formula architecture

Why one formula, three fragrances.

Consider how Diptyque approaches its candles. The same quality of wax, the same wick, the same burn performance — and then Baies, and Feu de Bois, and Tubereuse. The container is consistent. The identity changes. The standard does not move.

That is exactly the logic behind the Gentle Daily Shampoo family. The base formula is identical across all three variants. 99.5% Natural Origin Index per ISO 16128-2. Probiotic preservation via Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate. Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate as the primary mild surfactant — coconut-derived, gentle on canine skin. Hydrolyzed Oat Protein for coat conditioning. Tocopherol (Vitamin E) as antioxidant. This does not change.

What changes is the fragrance. Santal and White Tea for warmth and calm. Fig and Neroli for brightness and a quality of southern light. Hinoki and Bamboo for quiet, mineral precision. Three different sensory decisions for three different types of pet parent. One formula standard behind all of them.

The choice of variant is a fragrance preference, not a formula gamble. Whichever you choose, the base is the same — certified, probiotic preserved, vet-consulted.
