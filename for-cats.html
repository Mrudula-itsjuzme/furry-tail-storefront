Why these work for cats

Natural-origin. Gentle.
Formulated with care.

The Gentle Daily Shampoo range uses a natural-origin surfactant base — Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate and Cocamidopropyl Betaine, both coconut-derived — with a probiotic preservation system and no synthetic preservatives. The formula is gentle on skin and coat, and rinses clean without residue.

The same formula standard that makes these shampoos right for dogs makes them right for cats. If your household has both, one bottle works for both. Rinse thoroughly, avoid contact with eyes and mouth, and consult your vet for kittens under 12 weeks.

Dogs only

The rest of the range
is for dogs only.

The remaining four products use fragrance actives or functional ingredients that are not suitable for cats. This is noted clearly on each product page.

Anti-Tick & Flea Spray
Vetiver & Cypress

Uses vetiver root oil, cypress oil, and citronella — plant-derived actives with terpene profiles that are not safe for cats.

Paw Cleaner
Spearmint & Sea Salt

Contains Mentha Spicata Leaf Oil (spearmint). Menthol compounds in spearmint are not safe for cats to inhale or absorb through paw pads.

Dry Foam Shampoo
Mimosa & Tonka

The fragrance profile uses coumarin-bearing tonka bean extract. Formulated for dogs only.

Refreshing Mist
Yuzu & White Musk

Contains Citrus Junos Peel Oil (yuzu). Citrus terpenes at misting concentrations are not appropriate for cats. Formulated for dogs only.
