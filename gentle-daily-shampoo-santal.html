Description

The Gentle Daily Shampoo in Santal & White Tea is the warm, grounding option in the Ritual family. Formulated for frequent use, the surfactant system is gentle enough for weekly bathing without stripping the coat's natural oils. The 99.5% Natural Origin Index is calculated under ISO 16128-2 — not a marketing estimate, a certified number. The primary surfactant is Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate, coconut-derived and mild on canine skin pH. The probiotic preservation system replaces every synthetic preservative in the formula.

Key Ingredients
  • Santalum Album Oil (Santal) — fragrance primary. Steam-distilled from aged heartwood. Warm, dry, grounding.
  • Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract (White Tea) — antioxidant. Rich in polyphenols, supports skin barrier health.
  • Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate — mild surfactant, coconut-derived. Gentle lather, kind to canine skin pH.
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine — secondary surfactant, foam booster. Coconut-derived.
  • Hydrolyzed Oat Protein — coat conditioning. Smooths and strengthens hair fibre.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation. Fermentation-derived antimicrobial, replaces synthetic preservatives entirely.
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E) — antioxidant. Protects formula and supports coat health.
How to Use

Wet coat thoroughly. Apply a small amount and work into a rich lather from neck to tail. Leave for one to two minutes. Rinse completely. Towel dry or finish with a low-heat dryer. Suitable for weekly or bi-weekly bathing.

Safe For

Suitable for dogs and cats. Avoid contact with eyes and mouth. Rinse thoroughly after use. Suitable for animals over 12 weeks. Consult your vet for puppies or kittens under 12 weeks, or animals with known skin sensitivities.
