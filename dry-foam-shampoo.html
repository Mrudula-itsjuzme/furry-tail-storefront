Description

The Dry Foam Shampoo is a waterless, no-rinse formula for freshening the coat between bath days. Shake, spray, work through with hands or a brush, allow to dry. No water. No towelling. The foam absorbs odour and oil, leaving the coat refreshed. Mimosa & Tonka is the warmer, richer option in the Refresh family — powdery florals, tonka's vanilla-coumarin warmth. A between-bath ritual that feels like one.

Key Ingredients
  • Acacia Dealbata Flower Extract (Mimosa) — fragrance primary. Powdery, warm, floral.
  • Dipteryx Odorata Seed Extract (Tonka Bean) — fragrance secondary. Vanilla and coumarin warmth. IFRA-compliant.
  • Isobutane — propellant. Delivers the foam dispense format.
  • Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate — mild surfactant. Absorbs oil and odour without water.
  • Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein — coat conditioning. Helps smooth the coat shaft with each use.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation.
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E) — antioxidant.
How to Use

Shake well. Hold 20cm from the coat and spray evenly, working section by section. Use hands or a soft brush to distribute through the coat. Focus on areas that tend to build odour — behind the ears, under the collar, around the hindquarters. Allow to dry completely. No rinsing required. Use between bath days as needed.

Safe For

Formulated for dogs. Not suitable for cats. See our cat safety note for full detail on species safety.
