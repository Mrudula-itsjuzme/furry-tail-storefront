Description

The Anti-Tick & Flea Spray uses plant-derived actives — vetiver root oil, cypress oil, citronella, and neem extract — to help deter ticks and fleas through scent masking and natural insect-repellent properties. It is not a pesticide. It does not use synthetic chemical actives. Apply before every walk in tick or flea-active environments — parks, trails, monsoon-wet grass, anywhere cover is dense and damp. Allow to dry completely before contact with other animals or surfaces.

Key Ingredients
  • Vetiveria Zizanoides Root Oil (Vetiver) — fragrance primary, deterrent. Distilled from roots. Deep, earthy, persistent. Naturally repellent to certain insects.
  • Cupressus Sempervirens Oil (Cypress) — fragrance secondary, deterrent. Clean, resinous, woody. IFRA-compliant.
  • Cymbopogon Nardus Oil (Citronella) — deterrent active. Recognised natural insect-repellent compound.
  • Azadirachta Indica Leaf Extract (Neem) — deterrent active. Helps disrupt tick and flea activity through natural limonoid compounds.
  • Alcohol Denat. — carrier. Ensures even distribution and rapid dry-down.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation.
How to Use

Shake well. Spray evenly over the coat from 15 to 20cm distance, paying attention to legs, belly, and neck. Avoid eyes, nose, and mouth. Allow to dry completely before allowing contact with other animals or surfaces. Use before every walk in tick-active environments.

Safe For

Formulated for dogs. Not suitable for cats. Do not apply near eyes, nose, or mouth. If your dog has skin sensitivities, apply a small amount to a small area first and monitor for 24 hours. For established tick or flea infestations, consult your vet.
