Description

The Gentle Daily Shampoo in Fig & Neroli is the brighter, more expressive option in the Ritual family. The fragrance leads with sun-warmed fig and lifts with neroli from bitter orange blossom — together they create something with a quality of southern European warmth. The base formula is identical to all Gentle Daily Shampoo variants: 99.5% Natural Origin Index per ISO 16128-2, probiotic preservation, coconut-derived surfactants, gentle enough for weekly bathing.

Key Ingredients
  • Ficus Carica Fruit Extract (Fig) — fragrance primary. Warm, sweet, resinous green note.
  • Citrus Aurantium Amara Flower Oil (Neroli) — fragrance secondary. Cold-pressed from bitter orange blossom. Bright, floral, distinctly southern.
  • Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate — mild surfactant, coconut-derived. Gentle lather, kind to canine skin pH.
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine — secondary surfactant, foam booster. Coconut-derived.
  • Hydrolyzed Oat Protein — coat conditioning.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation. Fermentation-derived antimicrobial, replaces synthetic preservatives entirely.
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E) — antioxidant.
How to Use

Wet coat thoroughly. Apply a small amount and work into a rich lather from neck to tail. Leave for one to two minutes. Rinse completely. Towel dry or finish with a low-heat dryer. Suitable for weekly or bi-weekly bathing.

Safe For

Suitable for dogs and cats. Avoid contact with eyes and mouth. Rinse thoroughly after use. Suitable for animals over 12 weeks. Consult your vet for puppies or kittens under 12 weeks, or animals with known skin sensitivities.
