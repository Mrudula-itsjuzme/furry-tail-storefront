In full

Each ingredient, explained.

Fig
Ficus Carica Fruit Extract
Fragrance primary Fruit extract Fig & Neroli

Derived from Ficus carica fruit. Fig brings the rounded body in the Fig & Neroli shampoo: soft green skin, ripe fruit, and a lightly milky warmth that keeps the fragrance from becoming purely floral. It gives the formula a sun-warmed character while Neroli provides the bright lift. Used within the wash-off fragrance profile and balanced to stay gentle on the coat.

Sandalwood / Santal
Santalum Album Oil
Fragrance primary Steam-distilled IFRA-compliant

Steam-distilled from aged Santalum album heartwood. One of the most documented aromatic materials in personal care — warm, woody, smooth, with a dry sandalwood finish that is immediately recognisable. In the Santal & White Tea shampoo, it is the primary fragrance note. The IFRA-compliant usage level ensures the fragrance profile is safe for dogs and appropriate for the wash-off format. Sandalwood has been used in personal care for centuries and carries one of the strongest safety profiles of any fragrance material.

Neroli
Citrus Aurantium Amara Flower Oil
Fragrance primary Cold-pressed blossom IFRA-compliant

Extracted from the blossoms of the bitter orange tree (Citrus aurantium). Cold-pressed rather than steam-distilled, which preserves the brighter, more delicate floral top notes. Neroli is distinctly southern European in character — floral without being sweet, bright without being sharp. In the Fig & Neroli shampoo, it provides the high-note lift that keeps the fragrance from reading as simply fruity. IFRA-compliant at the concentration used.

Vetiver
Vetiveria Zizanoides Root Oil
Fragrance primary Root-distilled Dogs only

Distilled from the roots of Vetiveria zizanoides, a grass native to India. The root distillation — rather than leaf — gives vetiver its characteristic deep, earthy, smoky quality. It is a grounding note, not a green one. In Anti-Tick & Flea Spray, it contributes to the natural insect-deterrent profile alongside cypress and citronella. The earthy, persistent character also means the spray does not smell like a chemical deterrent — it smells like an intentional fragrance decision. Formulated for dogs only.

Spearmint
Mentha Spicata Leaf Oil
Active note Deodorising Dogs only

Steam-distilled from Mentha spicata leaves. Spearmint is cooler and softer than peppermint — the menthol content is lower, which makes it less sharp and more refreshing in application. In Paw Cleaner, it provides the cooling sensation on application and works as a natural deodorising agent. Note: spearmint contains menthol and carvone compounds that cats cannot safely metabolise. The Paw Cleaner is formulated for dogs only as a result.

Probiotic Preservative
Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate
Preservation system Fermentation-derived All products

The preservation system in all Furry Tail products. Derived from the fermentation of Leuconostoc bacteria with radish root. The fermentation process produces antimicrobial peptides that protect the formula against microbial contamination — the same function that synthetic preservatives like MIT, MCIT, parabens, and phenoxyethanol serve. The difference is chemistry: the fermentation-derived route replaces every synthetic preservative entirely. It requires more rigorous pH control in formulation and a narrower operating range than synthetics, which is why most brands do not use it. We chose it because the person we are building for would not want the alternative if they read the label.

White Tea
Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract
Antioxidant Skin barrier

Extracted from the youngest leaves and buds of the Camellia sinensis plant. White tea carries the highest polyphenol concentration of any tea extract — the minimal processing preserves the EGCG and catechin content that makes it effective as an antioxidant. In the Santal & White Tea shampoo, white tea extract contributes to skin barrier support and complements the sandalwood fragrance with its own quiet, clean character. It does not scent the product significantly — its role is functional, with the name earned by its presence on the ingredient deck.

Yuzu
Citrus Junos Peel Oil
Fragrance primary Japanese citrus IFRA-compliant

Cold-pressed from the peel of Citrus junos, a Japanese citrus fruit. Yuzu sits between lemon and grapefruit in profile — brighter than grapefruit, warmer than lemon, with a slightly tart, floral edge that makes it immediately distinctive. In Refreshing Mist, it is the lead fragrance note, paired with white musk to give the mist a clean, airy finish. Citrus top notes are designed for exactly this format: the burst of the spray, the dry-down, the lasting impression.

Vitamin E
Tocopherol
Antioxidant All products

A fat-soluble antioxidant present in all Furry Tail formulas. Tocopherol serves a dual function: it protects the formula from oxidation during shelf life, and it provides topical antioxidant benefit to the skin and coat. In rinse-off products like shampoos, the coat-benefit is limited but present. In leave-on products like the Paw Cleaner, Dry Foam Shampoo, and Refreshing Mist, the prolonged contact allows more meaningful conditioning benefit. It is present in every formula as a formulation decision, not a marketing claim.
