The formula difference

Three things most pet care brands skip.
We did not.

The 99.5% is certified. Not estimated.

Most brands that say “natural” are using a loosely defined term. Ours is calculated under ISO 16128-2, the international standard for natural origin measurement. Every batch has a number that can be verified. That is what certified means.

We replaced the preservatives. All of them.

Standard pet care uses MIT, MCIT, parabens, or phenoxyethanol to keep formulas shelf-stable. We use a probiotic system — Leuconostoc / Radish Root Ferment — that does the same job without any of those. It is a harder formula to build. We built it anyway.

Fine fragrance, not cover-up scent.

Santal & White Tea. Fig & Neroli. Hinoki & Bamboo. Vetiver & Cypress. Mimosa & Tonka. Yuzu & White Musk. Spearmint & Sea Salt. IFRA-compliant fragrance profiles built from the same raw material vocabulary as premium personal care. Part of the product identity.
