The Standard

What we mean when we say natural.

Three commitments. Each one is verifiable. None of them is optional.

99.5% Natural Origin Index per ISO 16128-2

ISO 16128-2 is the international standard for defining and calculating natural origin indices in cosmetic and personal care products. It is published by the International Organisation for Standardisation and is used by formulators and certifiers in Europe, Japan, the US, and India to establish a common language for what "natural origin" actually means. The standard defines the rules for attributing natural origin status to ingredients and provides a method for calculating the percentage of the formula that qualifies.

The Natural Origin Index is calculated on a weight-by-weight basis. Every ingredient in the formula is assessed against the standard's definitions: water-origin, mineral-origin, plant-origin, and various processing-derived categories. The result is a number — the percentage of the total formula weight that qualifies as naturally derived. For Furry Tail, that number is 99.5%. The remaining 0.5% is disclosed and accounted for.

Most brands that use "natural" as a marketing claim have never calculated what that means under a defined standard. Ours is a number. Per batch. Verifiable against the raw material declarations and the formula spec sheet. That is the difference between a claim and a commitment.

Probiotic preservation

Preservatives are necessary. Any water-based personal care product without an effective preservative system will develop microbial contamination — bacteria, yeast, mould — within days to weeks. The question is not whether to preserve, but how. The standard answer in the industry has been synthetic preservatives: methylisothiazolinone (MIT), methylchloroisothiazolinone (MCIT), parabens, phenoxyethanol, imidazolidinyl urea, and similar compounds. These are effective, stable, and cheap. They are also increasingly avoided by consumers who read labels on their own personal care products.

Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate is a fermentation-derived antimicrobial. It is produced by fermenting radish root with Leuconostoc bacteria — the same bacteria used in the fermentation of kimchi and other traditional fermented foods. The fermentation produces antimicrobial peptides that inhibit the growth of bacteria and yeast without the synthetic chemistry of conventional preservative systems. It provides the same shelf stability as conventional preservatives when formulated correctly.

Correctly is the operative word. The fermentation-derived system has a narrower effective pH range than synthetic alternatives. It requires more precise formulation control, more rigorous quality testing, and more consistent raw material sourcing. It is harder to work with. It is also the only way to replace every synthetic preservative in the formula — not most of them, all of them. That is what we did. Every Furry Tail product uses this system as its sole preservation mechanism.

We replaced every synthetic preservative. Not most of them. All of them.

IFRA-compliant fine fragrance

IFRA is the International Fragrance Association — the global industry body that sets fragrance safety standards. IFRA guidelines define maximum usage levels for fragrance ingredients across different product categories, based on toxicology data, exposure modelling, and safety reviews. Compliance with IFRA guidelines is the industry's baseline safety standard for any product that carries a fine fragrance.

Most pet care products that include fragrance use generic functional scents — "fresh", "clean", "botanical" — with no coherent olfactory identity and no reference to a fragrance standard. Our approach is different. Each Furry Tail fragrance is a named, IFRA-compliant identity built around specific fragrance materials. Santal and White Tea. Vetiver and Cypress. Hinoki and Bamboo. Fig and Neroli. Mimosa and Tonka. Spearmint and Sea Salt. Yuzu and White Musk. Each profile has been constructed within IFRA limits for the product format and application method.

Vet-consulted formulation

The Furry Tail formulas were reviewed by practising veterinarians before finalisation. This covered species eligibility (confirming which products are suitable for cats and which are dogs-only), surfactant type and concentration relative to canine and feline skin pH, fragrance ingredient review against known species sensitivities, and pH management for the probiotic preservation system. The Gentle Daily Shampoo range is confirmed suitable for both dogs and cats. Vet-consulted is not a marketing claim. It is a formulation practice that precedes launch and continues with any revision.
