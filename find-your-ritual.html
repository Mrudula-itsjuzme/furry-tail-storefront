The right products.

Not every dog needs the same ritual. Tell us about your pet's week and we will tell you where to start.

Bath day is the priority. For the dog who needs a thorough clean.

The Gentle Daily Shampoo is the foundation of any ritual. Choose the fragrance that suits your sensibility — Santal & White Tea for warmth, Fig & Neroli for brightness, Hinoki & Bamboo for quiet. All three share the same formula: 99.5% Natural Origin Index per ISO 16128-2, probiotic preservation, gentle surfactant system. The fragrance is the only thing that changes.

Gentle enough for weekly or bi-weekly use without stripping the coat's natural oils. The ritual that earns its name.

Santal & White Tea → Fig & Neroli → Hinoki & Bamboo →
Ticks and fleas are a concern. For dogs who walk in parks, trails, or monsoon-wet grass.

The Anti-Tick & Flea Spray goes on before every walk. Plant-derived actives — vetiver root oil, cypress oil, citronella, neem extract — help deter ticks and fleas through scent masking and natural insect-repellent properties. It is not a pesticide. It does not use synthetic chemical actives. It does not smell like it is trying to do a job.

Vetiver & Cypress, IFRA-compliant fragrance. Spray before the walk, not after. Allow to dry completely before contact with other animals or surfaces. Use consistently in tick-active environments — parks, trails, and anywhere grass is long and damp.

Anti-Tick & Flea Spray →
Paws are taking the city's punishment. Hot pavements. Monsoon mud. City grit.

The Paw Cleaner is the ritual at the door. A rinse-free foam formula that cleans each pad and the spaces between the toes without rinsing. Apply, massage, wipe off with a damp cloth. Two minutes. No tub required.

Spearmint & Sea Salt — cooling on application, naturally deodorising, and finished with a clean sea-salt note. The surfactant system is mild enough for daily use. After every walk, before they come inside. That is the ritual this product was built for.

Paw Cleaner →
Between baths is where the smell builds. For dogs who need a refresh without a full bath.

The Dry Foam Shampoo and Refreshing Mist are the between-bath system. They are designed to work together or independently, depending on how much of a refresh your dog needs between bath days.

The Dry Foam is the more thorough option: spray, work through with hands or a brush, allow to dry. No water, no rinsing, no towelling. It addresses both odour and oil, leaving the coat genuinely fresh. Mimosa & Tonka — warm, powdery, persistent. The Refreshing Mist is lighter: three sprays over the back and flanks, allow to settle. Yuzu & White Musk — clean, citrus-forward, airy. Use it alone as a quick between-bath lift, or as a finish after the Dry Foam.
