Why we exist

happily ever after.

We noticed a gap.
Then we closed it.

The gap we noticed.

The conscious consumer exists. The product for their dog did not.

The same person who reads their serum ingredient label before buying it, who knows the difference between a probiotic and a prebiotic, who has an opinion about preservative systems in their own skincare — that person has been buying whatever is on the pet care shelf. Not because they do not care. Because nothing was built for them.

Pet care in India (and largely, globally) has been either clinical or gimmicky. Clinical: veterinary-grade, no sensory consideration, effective but joyless. Gimmicky: neem-and-tulsi craft brands with claims that would not survive a single ISO audit, sold on the idea of nature rather than the fact of it.

The premium segment — natural origin, vet-consulted formulas, fine fragrance identities, honest certification — did not exist for pets the way it exists for people. That was the gap. Furry Tail is what sits in it.

What we built.

A formula standard that does not explain itself with asterisks.

The 99.5% Natural Origin Index is a calculated number, not an estimated one. It is derived per ISO 16128-2, the international standard for natural origin measurement. Every ingredient is accounted for. The 0.5% is disclosed. There is no marketing flexibility in it.

The preservation system uses Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — a fermentation-derived antimicrobial that replaces synthetic preservatives entirely. No MIT, no MCIT, no parabens, no phenoxyethanol. It was harder to formulate with. It has a narrower pH window. It requires more rigorous quality control. We chose it because the person we are building for would not want the alternative if they read the label.

The fragrances are IFRA-compliant fine fragrance identities. Not "fresh" or "lavender fields." Santal and White Tea. Vetiver and Cypress. Hinoki and Bamboo. Each is a coherent olfactory decision, designed for the home as much as for the dog.

Why it matters.

We did not build the first pet shampoo. We built the first one that respected the person buying it.

Every decision in this product is a design decision. The scent that opens when you uncap the bottle. The weight of the label copy and the precision of its claims. The choice to name an ingredient by its common form and its INCI designation both, so you know exactly what you are using. None of it is decoration. It is all the product. The experience of choosing it, using it, and keeping it on your shelf is as considered as the formula inside.

The person this brand was made for already existed before Furry Tail did. They read ingredient labels. They choose their own skincare with deliberate attention. They have a standard for what enters their home and a quiet frustration with anything that falls short of it. For their dog, nothing had been built to that standard. Not because that person was rare, but because no one had decided they were worth building for. Furry Tail is that decision.

Made in India. Not as a provenance story, not as a claim that needs explaining. The formulation rigour is the same. The fragrance compliance is the same. The 99.5% Natural Origin Index per ISO 16128-2 is the same standard, held the same way, regardless of where the bottle was filled. This is where we make it. It is good enough to be made here.
