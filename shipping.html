Shipping & Returns

Clear terms.
No surprises.

Delivery

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days across India. Express delivery (1 to 2 business days) is available at checkout for most pin codes. Free standard shipping is applied automatically on all orders above ₹999. Below that, a flat shipping fee applies — visible at checkout before payment.

All orders are dispatched via Shiprocket partner couriers and include a tracking number sent to your email once the order is picked up.

Processing time

Orders placed before 2pm IST on Monday through Friday are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2pm IST, or on weekends and public holidays, are processed on the next working day.

During sale periods or product launches, processing time may extend by one to two business days. We will communicate any delays directly by email.

Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for products that are unopened and unused, in their original condition. To initiate a return, email hello@furrytailjoy.com with your order number and the reason for return. We will provide a return shipping label and process the refund within 5 business days of receiving the returned item.

Opened products are not eligible for return unless they are faulty or incorrectly supplied. We assess each case individually and will not leave a customer without a resolution if there is a genuine fault with the product.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, email us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@furrytailjoy.com with your order number and a photograph of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you — no questions, no return required for damaged goods.

International shipping

International shipping is not available in Phase 1. All orders are dispatched within India only. Southeast Asia, Middle East, and UK launches are planned for Phase 2. If you are outside India and would like to be notified when international shipping opens, email hello@furrytailjoy.com or join the newsletter below.
