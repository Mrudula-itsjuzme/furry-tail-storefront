Description

The Paw Cleaner is a rinse-free foam formula for cleaning paw pads after walks. The surfactant system is mild enough for daily use — gentle on skin, effective on city grit, monsoon mud, and pavement residue. No water required, no tub required. Apply at the door before your dog comes inside. Spearmint & Sea Salt provides a naturally deodorising, cooling finish that works as well on hot-pavement days as on monsoon-mud evenings.

Key Ingredients
  • Mentha Spicata Leaf Oil (Spearmint) — active note. Naturally deodorising, cooling on application. Softer than peppermint.
  • Maris Sal accord (Sea Salt) — aroma accord. Clean, marine freshness. Not mineralised salt — an aroma profile that evokes clean sea air.
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine — mild surfactant. Foam former, effective on dirt and grit without stripping skin.
  • Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice — soothing. Calms skin on application, particularly useful after hot-pavement exposure.
  • Panthenol (Vitamin B5) — skin barrier support. Helps maintain pad hydration with daily use.
  • Leuconostoc/Radish Root Ferment Filtrate — probiotic preservation.
How to Use

Pump a small amount directly onto each paw or onto a damp cloth. Gently massage each pad and between the toes. Wipe off with a clean damp cloth. No rinsing required. Use after every walk.

Safe For

Formulated for dogs. Not suitable for cats — spearmint contains menthol and carvone compounds that cats cannot safely metabolise. See our cat safety note.
